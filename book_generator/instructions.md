# How To Play

Each puzzle page has a grid of letters and a list of words hidden
somewhere inside it. Your job is to find every word on the list.

## Rules

- {{directions}}
- {{backwards}}
- Letters can be shared between two words that cross paths

## Tips

- Circle or highlight each word as you find it
- Cross words off the list below the grid as you go
- {{scan_tip}}
- Stuck on a word? Look for uncommon letters first, like Q, X, or Z

## Checking Your Work

Every puzzle's solution is shown in the answer key section at the back of
the book, grouped four to a page for quick reference.

Good luck, and have fun!
